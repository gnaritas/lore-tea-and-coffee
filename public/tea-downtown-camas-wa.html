✦ Tea & Teas · Camas, WA

Tea in downtown Camas — half the name, half the counter

A tea house that happens to pull espresso, in a house a century old. Tea here isn't a box in the back room: it's in the name over the door.

The tea end of the counter

LORE: Tea and Coffee was named for both halves of the counter. When you come in, ask what's steeping — the tea list is the answer you'll get from the people who own the place, Christian and Tara Kelly, who took this downtown shop over from Hidden River Coffee Roasters and kept it a house of hot drinks in the old sense of the word.

Slow mornings suit tea best, so Sundays open at 8 and run till 3 — the day to bring a book, a friend, or nowhere else to be. The rest of the week the kettle's on from 7, and on Fridays it stays on through Cocktail Nights until 9.

Questions people ask

Do you really serve tea, or is it just the name?

Both — the name is the promise. Hot tea is on the counter seven days a week; ask what's on the list today when you come in.

Can I get tea to go?

Yes. Call (360) 980-9210 ahead of time — curbside and in-store pickup are both available.

Which day is best for a slow tea morning?

Sunday, 8 AM to 3 PM, in a century-old house on NE 5th Avenue. Bring a book.

Where do you get your coffee, then?

Hidden River Coffee Roasters, the same roastery this building housed — see our coffee page.

Come for the tea. Stay for the story.

LGBTQ+ friendly, seven days a week, downtown Camas.
