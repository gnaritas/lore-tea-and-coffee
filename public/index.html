Chapter One

Once upon a time, in a century-old house on NE 5th Avenue…

Christian and Tara Kelly — high-school sweethearts who married young, raised four kids, worked hard, and made their way to Washington State — took their chance on a shop of their own this fall, in the same downtown building Hidden River Coffee Roasters called home before LORE opened.

Nothing here is a clean slate. The house is a century old, the coffee is the local roast Camas already drinks, and the story is just under a new name: LORE: Tea and Coffee. Christian and Tara write the next chapter one cup at a time — lattes in the morning, tea in the afternoon, and cocktails on Friday nights, when the shop stays open until 9.

Stop by, call ahead for curbside, or read on about what we pour.
