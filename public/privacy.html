What this site collects

The contact form asks for your name, phone, email (optional), and message. That's it. When you submit, the note is emailed to the shop so someone can answer you. It is used to reply — not for anything else.

Cookies & trackers

These pages set no cookies of their own and load no advertising or analytics trackers. The embedded Google Maps frame (on the home, tea, and contact pages) is served by Google, which applies its own privacy policies to that frame.
