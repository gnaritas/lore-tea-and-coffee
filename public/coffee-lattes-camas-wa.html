Coffee & lattes in downtown Camas, poured from 7 AM

The espresso machine in this building has steam on its record — and the roaster behind the cup is the one this building is known for.

The coffee: Hidden River, where you're standing

LORE: Tea and Coffee serves coffee from Hidden River Coffee Roasters — the specialty roastery that made this century-old house on NE 5th Avenue its home until Christian and Tara Kelly bought the business in June 2026. When LORE opened in October, the coffee stayed the same; only the name out front changed.

That means your latte habit survives the rename. Lattes, brewed coffee, and the rest of the counter run on the same local roast, poured from 7 every morning except Sunday, when we open at 8.

Questions people ask

Whose coffee do you serve?

Hidden River Coffee Roasters — Camas's own specialty roaster, and the coffee this building is known for. The shop's name is new; the roast isn't.

Can I order ahead for curbside?

Yes. Call (360) 980-9210 with your order and name; we'll bring it out, or you can grab it at the counter.

What are your morning hours?

7 AM Monday through Saturday (until 5 PM, or 9 PM on Friday for Cocktail Nights), and 8 AM to 3 PM on Sunday.

Is it just coffee?

No — tea is half the name. See our tea page, and ask at the counter about Friday's Cocktail Nights.
