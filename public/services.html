✦ The Menu You'll Find at the Counter

What we serve, downtown Camas

A coffee house and a tea house in one century-old building — morning roasts, afternoon tea, and cocktails to start the weekend on Friday night.

✦  Coffee & Lattes

Every cup starts with Hidden River Coffee Roasters — the Camas roastery that lived in this very building until Christian and Tara Kelly took the shop over. If you've drunk a latte from this counter for years, you'll recognize the roast; the name out front is what's new.

Mornings open at 7 AM, seven days a week. Call ahead and we'll set your cup aside for curbside or counter pickup.

✦  Tea & Teas

Tea is half the name and half the counter. Ask at the counter what's on the list today. Slow Sunday mornings start at 8 and run till 3.

✦  Friday Cocktail Nights

Every Friday, LORE brings back its downtown unwinding hour: Cocktail Nights, cocktails from the counter, the shop open until 9 PM. Come as you are — this is a LGBTQ+ friendly house, and downtown Camas does Fridays well.

Curbside & in-store pickup

Order ahead by phone and your drinks wait at the counter — curbside and in-store pickup both work. Call (360) 980-9210 with your name and what you'd like; big order for a table of friends? Give us a little more warning and we'll stage it.

Hungry for one of these? The counter is open.
